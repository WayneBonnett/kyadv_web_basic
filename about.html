About Kentucky ADV

Your source for adventure riding in the Bluegrass State

Our Story

Kentucky ADV is your go-to resource for adventure motorcycle riding in Kentucky. We're passionate about exploring the beautiful backroads, gravel paths, and hidden gems that make Kentucky a perfect destination for adventure riders.

What We Offer

  • Detailed route guides and GPX files for your next adventure
  • Adventure riding tips and techniques for all skill levels
  • Gear reviews and recommendations for your setup
  • Scenic ride reports from across Kentucky
  • Community meetups and group rides

Whether you're a beginner looking to start your adventure riding journey or an experienced rider seeking new routes, we're here to help you discover the best that Kentucky has to offer.
